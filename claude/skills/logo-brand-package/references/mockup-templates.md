# Mockup Templates — Proportions and Placement

Flat vector scenes, not photo composites. Each template is a simple outline/silhouette drawn in SVG with the logo placed inside a defined safe zone.

**Business card** — 350x200 canvas (standard card ratio). Logo occupies the top-left third, max width 40% of card width, with 24px margin from both edges. Background: flat color, or the brand pattern at low opacity (10-15%) behind a solid-color logo area.

**Apparel/tote** — 400x500 canvas, outline of a tote bag or t-shirt silhouette (simple closed path, no texture). Logo centered horizontally, vertical position at roughly 35% from top, max width 35% of garment width.

**Signage** — 600x300 canvas, a flat rectangle or rounded-rect "sign" shape with a contrasting background fill. Logo centered, max width 50% of sign width, generous clear space (at least one logo-height of padding on all sides).

**Social avatar** — 400x400 canvas, circular crop. Logo centered, max width/height 60% of the circle's diameter — use the icon-only lockup, not the full horizontal one; avatars are square/circular and crop kills wide marks.

General rule: never let the logo touch a template edge. Minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of its tallest letterform or icon element.
